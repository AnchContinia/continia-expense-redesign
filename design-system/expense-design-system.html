No new colour — Performance Purple carries "stop"
Rejection, over-policy amounts, failed OCR and validation errors need a colour that reads as "stop" rather than "caution". Rather than introduce a red the brand doesn't have, Performance Purple #983EAE takes that job — it is already in the palette, already reserved for product differentiation, and sits beside navy without fighting it. Reserved for this meaning in the product: never decorative, never a large fill.

Approval status scale

Six states, one colour each, identical on mobile and desktop. Colour is never the only signal — every chip carries a label, and every chip carries an icon in dense contexts. Posted is the only filled chip: it reads as locked.

ChipMeansTokenWho acts
DRAFT Created, not yet submitted neutral Employee
PENDING Waiting in an approver's queue warning Manager → Controller
ON HOLD Parked by an approver pending information info Approver
APPROVED Cleared all approval steps, awaiting posting success Business Central
REJECTED Sent back with a reason — reason is mandatory danger Employee re-submits
POSTED Posted in Business Central. Read-only. posted Nobody
